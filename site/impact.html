How we measure

Proof,
not guesses.

Activity log

Every workshop and station is logged with the date, place, number of people, and volunteer hours.

Before & after survey

Students answer the same anonymous questions before a workshop and 4–6 weeks later, so we can see if habits change.

Reach online

Website visits and video views show how far the message travels beyond the room.
