Our checklist

Five questions
for any claim.

01 / SOURCE

Where is it
published?

Peer-reviewed journals and medical organizations (like the AAD) are more reliable than ads, influencers, or product pages.

02 / PEOPLE

Who was
studied?

Results from cells in a dish or animals don't always apply to people. Bigger studies with more people are usually stronger.

03 / CAUSE

Cause or
just a link?

"Linked to" doesn't mean "causes." Two things can happen together without one causing the other.

04 / MONEY

Who paid
for it?

A study funded by the company selling the product isn't automatically wrong — but it's worth noticing.

05 / BIG PICTURE

Do other studies
agree?

One study is a clue, not a conclusion. Trust builds when many studies point the same way.
