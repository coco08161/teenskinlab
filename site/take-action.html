Program 01

Sun-Smart
Workshops.

A free, interactive 30-minute session led by Teen Skin Lab volunteers. No cost, no sales pitch — just the facts, a game, and a plan.

Request a workshop →
  • Who it's forMiddle and high school classes, summer camps, youth sports teams, swim clubs, scout troops, and after-school programs.
  • What's coveredHow UV damages skin, how to read a sunscreen label, how much to use, and the ABCDE warning signs.
  • How it worksA short talk, a live "how much sunscreen?" demo, and a Myth Lab flip-card game. Everyone leaves with a handout and a QR code to this site.
  • What we ask30 minutes, a space to present, and permission for a short anonymous survey so we can measure what works.

FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Does a workshop cost anything?

No. Workshops are always free.

Who teaches the workshops?

Trained Teen Skin Lab student volunteers. Our materials are based on guidance from the American Academy of Dermatology and U.S. health agencies.

Do you give medical advice?

No. We teach general prevention and awareness. For questions about your own skin, talk with a trusted adult and a healthcare professional.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in South Florida. If you're farther away, reach out about starting a chapter.

How do you use survey answers?

Survey answers are anonymous and are only used in totals, like "X% of students now reapply sunscreen," to measure whether our programs work.
