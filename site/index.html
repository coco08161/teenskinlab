Our mission

Sun damage adds up.
So do good habits.

Skin cancer is the most common cancer in the U.S. — and one of the most preventable. In South Florida, strong UV is a year-round reality for teens at practice, PE, the beach, and the pool. We're working to make sun protection a normal habit before damage adds up.

1 in 5Americans will develop skin cancer by age 70.
Up to 80%of the sun's UV rays can pass through clouds.
2 hrsHow often to reapply sunscreen outdoors — and after swimming or sweating.
